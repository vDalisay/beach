# P25 — Beachfront and underwater presentation

Status: implemented and exercised in the playable beach on 23 September 2026. The six named views were recaptured with the same `restoration-fixture` seed before and after restoring the starter section. “After” means that one section is restored; it does not claim the full beach is clean.

The world now uses staged Synty sand edges, palms, shade sails, deck furniture, sandcastles, lifeguard lookout compositions, volleyball net, boards and boats, beach shops, skyline, dock and lighthouse. The glass item uses `SM_Prop_Drink_Bottle_01`, and the sorting station uses Synty outdoor table, bins and bag. All 28 item definitions have non-cube visuals: 25 Synty meshes/compositions, two project-owned rescue attachment shapes and one project-owned flat residue stain. The small rowboat is represented by the pack's RIB hull. A later pass replaced the portable tent's broad shade-sail composition with the pack's dedicated `SM_Prop_Shelter_02` model. Gameplay collisions, IDs, sorting space, placement slots and the backdrop boundary remain intact. The city is decorative and cannot be entered.

Rendering is Godot 4.6 Compatibility/OpenGL. A fixed sun has 180 m shadow distance; neutral ambient fill has energy 0.34. Warm sand uses staged `Sand_01.png` on one curved surface, hiding the old nine visible boxes and shore-slope seam while retaining their collision. A single authored curved water mesh follows the physical shoreline at y=0.08, with a project-owned material instance of the supplied Synty water shader: turquoise shallows, mild normals and shore foam, without a compute dependency or moving physical surface. Underwater camera tint/fog uses density 0.022, and the seabed has subtle animated caustics from a staged Synty noise texture. The underwater environment activates only when the camera submerges, avoiding double tint above water. The water shader remains surface-facing, so the underwater view is provided by the environment rather than a second translucent underside. Distant water can show a dark horizon band, and foam can look bright at some angles; neither blocks the gameplay surface or targets. MSAA was not separately tuned in this packet.

Playable setup/actions/observed: loaded the full beach and inspected the six saved reference positions in sunlight and underwater; the pier and lighthouse scene check found one lighthouse, 144 dock platform modules, 72 rail modules and intact collision. Started a seeded run with actual spawned waste and Synty item meshes. At the starter station, sorted 80 waste and 10 props through the real table/placement/deposit/truck flow; the section restored once, paid $15 and produced its receipt. The after captures show the starter-section litter removed and palm presentation changed, while UI, outlines and underwater target visibility remained readable. These scripted play actions use the real `RunRoot`, sorting, bag, truck and restoration components, not a parallel mock state. The full scene and assets imported without missing-material or shader errors. Observed checks: `P25_PIER ... failures=0`, `P20_COMPLETION ... failures=0`, broad boot/asset/ownership/input/manifest checks, and movement, placement, faint, scanner and save-physics flows all passed after the art changes.
