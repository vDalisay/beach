# C06 handoff — underwater palette checkpoint

## Shore and offshore water pass — 24 September 2026

The current water mesh now carries the authored `Coastline.shore_z()` value in UV2. Its existing Compatibility shader blends toward a darker offshore colour over 25–190 m from that shore, with a flank term for the nonplayable outer water. The mesh bounds, shore vertices, cosmetic wave settings and gameplay water level are unchanged. Material colours are shallow `(0.7, 0.8, 0.65)`, deep `(0.44, 0.63, 0.64)`, very deep `(0.14, 0.4, 0.42)` and offshore `(0.01, 0.15, 0.24)`; these are shader inputs, not displayed RGB values.

The [C06-v13 aerial initial](images/C06-v13/aerial-initial.png) and [restored](images/C06-v13/aerial-restored.png) views retain visible shallows and make farther water bluer than the [v5 baseline](images/C06-v5/aerial-initial.png). The [along-shore initial](images/C06-v13/along-shore-initial.png) and [restored](images/C06-v13/along-shore-restored.png) views show turquoise player-height water with the pier and lighthouse readable. A fixed initial pixel in that view changed from RGB `(218,255,255)` at v5 to `(66,186,200)` at v13; this is a local comparison, not a palette acceptance metric. The hard transition beyond the nonplayable outer sand tips is still visible in the elevated view. An attempted water perimeter extension produced larger triangular seams and was reverted.

The v13 paired run exited 0 in Godot 4.6.1 Compatibility with seed `full-run-integration`, 1920×1080, FOV 85, 5,400 waste, 300 props, one receipt/result, two reloads and zero state-invariant errors; see the [capture log](images/C06-v13/capture-log.txt). Native `tests/validate_pier_art.gd`, `tests/validate_faint.gd` and `tests/run_checks.gd` exited 0 after the shader edit. The faint pass exercised submergence, surface thresholds, refill and both oxygen tiers. A 600-frame rendered water-filled view at `(0,2.1,45) → (0,0,180)` on the developer Ryzen 5 5600 / RTX 3070 measured 6.04 ms average frame, 6.41 ms p95, 1.66 ms average physics, 1,711 maximum draw calls, 191.1 MB measured scene memory and 1,356.0 MB video memory. This is not the C09 target-hardware/export gate.

## Current result — 24 September 2026

The existing Compatibility underwater environment now uses a bluer background `(0.08, 0.29, 0.42)`, ambient colour `(0.32, 0.56, 0.68)` at energy `0.46`, and fog colour `(0.09, 0.32, 0.47)` at density `0.075`. The shared shore mesh retains its above-water white tint and uses `(0.95, 0.85, 1)` for the near submerged row and `(0.65, 0.75, 0.95)` for deeper rows. Geometry, collision, water level, oxygen logic and item positions are unchanged. The separate `seabed_caustics.tres` is not used by the current beach scene, so it was not changed.

The [C06-v4 reef initial](images/C06-v4/reef-initial.png) and [restored](images/C06-v4/reef-restored.png) captures use the same camera as the [earlier C04-v34 pair](images/C04-v34/capture-log.txt). The near litter remains distinct, the floor is warmer, and the distant boat becomes faint against blue fog. The restored state still exposes sparse upright coral proxies, a flat floor and a hard horizon. The [aerial initial](images/C06-v4/aerial-initial.png) shows that the tint change did not visibly alter the above-water bay composition. The paired capture used Godot 4.6.1 Compatibility, seed `full-run-integration`, 1920×1080 and FOV 85. Its accelerated setup finished 5,400 waste and 300 props, one receipt/result, two reloads and zero state-invariant errors; see the [capture log](images/C06-v4/capture-log.txt).

`tests/validate_world_traversal.gd` exited 0 in the main scene: 147 m route, three huts, both reef lanes, six new catalog items collected by aimed primary input, no overlap among 1,010 reef objective origins, and a real chair carried onto/off the pier. This confirms the changed tint mesh still supports the played route and pickups. It does not establish HUD readability or visual acceptance at 720p.

**C06 remains open.** Tune the above-water light, clouds, foam and shore/deep-water continuity; remove the conspicuous bounded water strips. Inspect water-surface crossings, near-item and HUD readability at 720p/1080p and both FOV extremes with normal input. Profile the expensive water view. C07 owns replacement habitat geometry and plants; the current coral proxies and flat underwater floor cannot pass the reef rubric by colour alone.
