# Compact beach revision — 23 September 2026

The user's quarter-size direction supersedes the earlier three-minute/640 m plan. The playable coastline, promenade, water bounds and end walls now span x = -80…80 m. The authored walking route is 148 m (the prior route was 631.1 m); no Synty model was downscaled to achieve it. The curved shore, city frontage, three service huts, shop, activity landmarks, pier and lighthouse were re-authored inside the shorter footprint. The nine disabled old sand boxes and flat seabed were removed from the scene, leaving the shared curved mesh/collision authoritative. Furniture terraces wrap into rows instead of spilling across neighbouring zones.

The 18 deterministic spawn packs use 16 × 48 cells at 0.7 m spacing. This keeps the original 5,700 required IDs and 40 optional valuables, with distinct physical poses. Pier moorings and east-coral packs were moved clear of the lighthouse island and pier head after an actual rescue-scene failure. Content version `beach-content-4` changes the manifest hash; existing saves remain on disk but are intentionally rejected as incompatible rather than loading old-world coordinates. The normal new-save and recovery flow passes on the compact build.
