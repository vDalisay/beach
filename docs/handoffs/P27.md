# P27 — Interaction polish and accessibility pass

Status: functional pass on 23 September 2026; the short silent-video acceptance clip is recorded, while physical-controller and physical 4K/TV gates remain open.

The playable placement lab now runs a 0.25 s snap, then a 1 → 1.08 → 1 visual-root pulse (0.125 s each). A completed prop family starts a 0.7 s mint shader sweep only after all its placed views exist. Each transition uses a temporary material overlay on that group's meshes; an unrelated prop does not share it. Removing and re-placing a member replays the sweep but never pays the $15 reward twice. Reduced motion removes the scale pulse and sweep while retaining the placement travel. Pickup travel is 0.24 s, bag collection travel 0.22 s, and throw launch is 8.0 m/s for small objects or 5.5 m/s for two-hand props, plus 1.2 m/s upward. Collision and saved transforms are never scaled for feedback.
