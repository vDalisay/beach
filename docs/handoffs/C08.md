At FOV 70 the hands were oversized and cropped at the bottom edge; at 110 the arms stretched into thin diagonals ending in small hands ([before/after](images/FIN05-open-items/fov-arms.png)). `HandRig` now keeps the framing of the default 85° ([plan 12, section 7](../plan/12-look-and-model-pass.md#7-model-review-and-open-item-follow-up--24-september-2026)):

  - the Synty arms, whose IK targets live in the same space
  - the fallback hands
  - each socket's visuals, now under a per-socket `View` node

  It follows the live camera FOV, so a settings change applies immediately.

- **Checks:** three checks now find the equipped tool under `ToolSocket/View`. The packed-release check also finds the tent through the project model's `Visual/beach_tent`, a contract that changed in the earlier model pass.

## Synty first-person arms and modelled tools — 24 September 2026

**Arms (A17):** `tools/blender/build_fp_arms.py` imports the licensed `PalmCityCharacters.fbx` and keeps `SM_Chr_Surfer_Male_01`'s two arms (236 vertices) skinned to the shared rig. - **Rig:** the model faces +Z, so it is turned 180°. View-model placement puts the shoulders ahead of and below the eye (outside the view) at 1.1× scale, so the palms reach the existing, already-tuned sockets.
- **IK:** a native `TwoBoneIK3D` (Shoulder → Elbow → Hand, extended 8 cm to the palm, poles down and out) moves each palm smoothly to the target for the current state:
  - bag in the left hand, gripped
  - tool in the right hand, gripped
  - small props in either hand, palm under the prop
  - two-handed carry, palms at the sides of the prop
- **Fingers:** curl about local Z per grip (relaxed, hold, grip). Right-side bones point along −X, which the IK end direction accounts for.
- **Material:** the converted Palm City polygon shader ignores the character skin mask, so `shaders/beach_character.gdshader` applies `Skin_01_Mask` with a tan skin colour.
- **Unchanged:** sockets, item presentation, input and saves. Arms cast no shadows, and the sorting table still hides the whole rig.

**Tools and gear:** new stick, vacuum, sand sifter, detector and cloth models. Long tools are carried grip-first with the working end reaching forward and down to the sand; per-tool transforms are in `progression.gd`.
