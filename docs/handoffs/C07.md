# C07 checkpoint — local reef fish reward

## Boid reef fish, modelled turtle behaviour and bubbles — 24 September 2026

Fish schools and turtles now use the low-poly A01 turtle and A02 reef fish models from the FIN-05 model pass (copied unchanged from the `asset-review-low-poly` worktree with `ModelLibrary`). Each school flocks as nine boids around its route leader: separation, alignment and cohesion with a rear blind spot, burst-and-glide cruising, level swimming, and seabed and surface margins. Blue tangs, yellow tangs and clownfish differ in speed, tightness and tail-beat rate. All fish in a school draw as one MultiMesh, and `fish_swim.gdshader` bends each body with a tail-beat wave and a C-curl when it turns. A looming swimmer triggers a flash expansion: the fish nearest the threat C-start away, the startle spreads through the school within about 0.1 s, and each darting fish throws a puff and short trail of bubbles before the school regroups. A calm, slow swimmer is gradually tolerated closer. A freshly restored school bursts out of the reef the same way; loading a restored reef does not replay that burst. Route loops are now centripetal Catmull-Rom curves, so fish and turtles travel smoothly through their corners instead of stopping at each point.

`TurtleAnimator` drives the model's four flipper joints, the head and tail (in `turtle_body.gdshader`) and the body from how the turtle actually moves. In water it swims with front-flipper power strokes, glides with the flippers swept back and sculls when hovering, and it banks into turns. On the beach it crawls with both front flippers together, pausing to rest and kicking sand. Entangled rescue turtles struggle in fits, and freed ones turn to face their route. Its stroke rhythm sets the route speed, so the turtle surges on each downstroke. `TurtleBehavior` (formerly the companion script) adds routines on top of the loop: surfacing to breathe with an exhale, grazing on the real bottom (found by raycast, so reef rock counts), and joining a nearby swimmer. While a swimmer is close, the turtle watches them, and its bubble trail still gives the 1.6× swim boost. Bubbles and sand are `ParticlePool` CPU particles that rise at size-dependent speeds, wobble and pop at the surface. No save data, objective, reward or collision changed.
